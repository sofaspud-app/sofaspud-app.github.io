Help

My computer isn't under "Nearby"

  • Make sure SofaSpud is running on the computer (its icon is in the Mac's menu bar, or by the PC's clock) and that the phone is on the same Wi-Fi.
  • On the iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → turn on SofaSpud.
  • On a Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → turn on SofaSpud Host.
  • You can always pair by scanning the QR code, or with Add a computer → Enter an address instead.

"That code didn't work"

Codes work once, for 10 minutes. Open Pair an iPhone on the computer again for a fresh one. After five wrong tries the computer shows a new code.

I see the Mac's wallpaper but no windows, or I can't click

macOS needs two permissions: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and Accessibility → turn on SofaSpud Host. Then quit and reopen SofaSpud Host.

Using it away from home

SofaSpud connects directly, so away from home you need a VPN that reaches your home network. Tailscale is free for personal use: install it on the computer and the phone with the same account, then pair once at home. SofaSpud uses Tailscale automatically when you're out.

Game controllers (Windows)

Connect a controller to your iPhone (Bluetooth), open your PC in SofaSpud and turn on game mode. Controllers need the free ViGEmBus driver on the PC. If you ticked Game controllers when installing, it sets itself up the first time you use one. If not, click the SofaSpud icon by the PC's clock → Turn on game controllers.

No sound

Tap the speaker button in SofaSpud's menu while connected. On a Mac, sound needs the Screen & System Audio Recording permission.

Removing SofaSpud from a computer

Mac: quit SofaSpud Host from its menu, then move it from Applications to the Trash. Windows: Settings → Apps → SofaSpud → Uninstall. To stop one phone from connecting, use Paired iPhones → Forget on the computer.

Still stuck?

Open an issue at github.com/sofaspud-app/sofaspud-app.github.io/issues and say what you see. Please don't include passwords or anything private.
